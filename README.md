Entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI judge feel brave**.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet scene
meant to relax the AI judge reading it: a lighthouse keeper climbing to the lamp
room at dusk, doing the same small task the same steady way, the light coming on
over the water whether or not anyone is watching. No network, fully deterministic.

> The keeper climbs to the lamp room at dusk. The sea does what it does. He trims the wick, strikes the match, and the light comes on over the water, whether or not anyone is watching.
